# respond-in-their-terms

**Trigger.** When writing a question, decision or result for the user.

**Rule.** Explain the user impact. For a decision, give options with tradeoffs and a recommendation with its reason.

**Enforcement.** This is guidance evaluated by human or agent review; AEP does not mechanically judge prose quality.

**Evidence basis.** Adopted as general engineering guidance for AEP 5.0. The release tests exercise the catalog and citation checks; they do not establish downstream occurrences of this principle. Project-specific observations belong in the project lesson store.
